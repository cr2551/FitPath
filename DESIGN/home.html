The calories card follows the reference layout: goal header, ring with macro legend, footer strip. Cards are light with no green block, the selected day is ink rather than orange, and the ring has wide gaps between segments. Data is illustrative; the phone scrolls. Full rules are in DESIGN/README.md.

Calorie ring

  • Arc length = eaten ÷ goal (1,640 / 2,100 = 78%). The grey track is what's left.
  • Segments = each macro's share of eaten calories, ordered protein → carbs → fat, clockwise from 12 o'clock, matching the legend order.
  • 10 px gaps (about 8°) at every boundary, including both ends of the track; each segment gives up half a gap per side so proportions stay exact.

Differences from the reference

  • Date tiles and buttons use an 8 px maximum radius instead of pills (project style rule).
  • Ring segments have square ends and wider, even gaps, plus an explicit "left" track.
  • The macro legend follows the ring order (protein, carbs, fat) and adds each macro's share of calories.
  • No profile photo or notification bell in the MVP: the header has profile and edit-layout buttons.
