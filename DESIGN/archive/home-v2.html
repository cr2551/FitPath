Home · v2

Athletic and fresh: forest green, lime energy, condensed scoreboard numbers, and macro colors protein red, carbs blue, fat yellow. Data is illustrative; the phone scrolls. Full rules are in DESIGN/README.md.

Calorie ring

  • Arc length = calories eaten ÷ goal (here 1,640 / 2,100 = 78%). The empty track is what's left.
  • Segments = the share of eaten calories from each macro, in the fixed order protein → carbs → fat, clockwise from 12 o'clock.
  • Square ends and 3 px gaps between segments; there are no rounded caps.

Layout decisions

  • One green hero block per screen; everything else is white cards on the light green background.
  • Log food (lime) and Start workout (green) sit right under the ring, because they're the two most frequent tasks.
  • The date strip lets you inspect another day; the selected day is lime and always visible, so past days are never mistaken for today.
  • Macro bars keep the spec's grams-vs-target progress and add the macro's share of calories, which ties them to the ring segments.
  • Shapes stay disciplined: 8 px max radius, square bar and arc ends, no gradients, no shadows.

Questions for the workshop

  • Carbs blue, or another hue (orange or green clash with other colors)?
  • Lime intensity: right amount of energy, or too loud?
  • Ring legend: % of calories (shown) or kcal per macro?
  • Keep both quick actions, or a single floating "Log" button?
